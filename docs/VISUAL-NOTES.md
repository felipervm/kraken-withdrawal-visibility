### Hero prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide website hero artwork for an independent study about clarity during secure withdrawal reviews. Create an elegant abstract 3D editorial still life, a translucent lavender glass arch surrounding a small luminous pearl, with three thin satin purple ribbons entering from different directions and aligning into one clear path through the arch. The forms evoke protection and communication continuity without literal locks or crypto coins. Deep aubergine background #120b26; vivid violet #7132f5, pale lilac #d9c9fa and soft white highlights. Matte and frosted glass textures, soft studio lighting, subtle grain, high-end restrained art direction. Wide landscape composition, sculpture concentrated on right half, left half very dark quiet negative space suitable for white headline overlay. Beautiful coherent forms, readable silhouette. No text, no letters, no logos, no dashboard, no watermark. Original artwork, not an official Kraken asset.

### Channel illustration prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape editorial illustration beside text in a research website about support communication consistency. Three softly rounded frosted glass panels float at different depths, linked by one continuous violet ribbon. Each panel has only a subtle abstract circular impression, no interface content or writing. One warm-white pearl follows the continuous path. Pale lilac studio background #efe9fa, rich purple #7132f5 edges, soft translucent lavender shadows. Sophisticated physical materials, sculptural 3D still life, soft diffuse lighting, subtle realistic grain, restrained composition with ample breathing room. Landscape 3:2 composition. Visual metaphor for one review appearing across app, email and support. No words, letters, numerals, logos, crypto coins, stock-chart arrows, locks, people or watermark. Original artwork, not an official Kraken asset.

## Editorial choices

Remove self-presentation labels and explicit job requests from the main page. Tell the actual research sequence in first person: reading guidance, following public reports, modeling checks and sketching a next step. Keep interest in Kraken in the closing context without claiming customer experiences, production outcomes or background not supplied by the author. Keep simulation disclosures near the analysis and source limits near the evidence; put detailed method boundaries in the research notes.
