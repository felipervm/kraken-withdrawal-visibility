# Operational decision brief — The Visibility Gap
**Question:** Which withdrawal review cases warrant attention first, without conflating routine holds, actual data mismatches and security-sensitive communication?

**Independent, fictional answer:** A six-case sample routes one to customer-visibility investigation, one to data quality, one to expected-hold guidance, two to policy review and one to monitoring. These are illustrative analyst routes, not Kraken policy or live classifications.

**Why this matters:** Aggregate repeat-contact rates mix fundamentally different populations. Compare manual reviews separately from scheduled holds. Require an approved authoritative state, channel timestamps, and privacy-approved next-step vocabulary before interpreting communication consistency.

**Potential decision:** Investigate cross-channel state conflicts first; route possible policy disclosures to Risk/Compliance; test permitted messaging only for manual-review cases with repeated contacts and missing approved next steps. No automated release, no inference of fraud status, and no fabricated SLA.

**Measure carefully:** primary proposed experiment metric = seven-day repeat-support-contact rate **per eligible manual-review case**. Segment by review type, risk-safe communication availability and channel, with guardrails on improper disclosure, complaints, support accessibility and operations escalations. Use a randomized or staggered approved rollout before making causal impact claims.
